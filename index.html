Every Drop Interactive

200 years in one catchment. Keep a river from 1850 to 2050 and watch your decisions re-plumb the water cycle itself — clearing, sewage, a dam, a sealed city, a changed climate. Consulting is real: some choices only appear once you've listened.

Share the River Interactive

“The Big Dry” — steer a Murray–Darling catchment through a five-year drought. Set policy by posture (not numbers), read the forecast odds, and live with an irreversible legacy of salt, aquifer and town. Ends with what really happened, plus an opt-in reflection.

A worked example, three ways. Model P·Q·E — pattern, quantify, exception — on the world's water at Grade 5–6, 7–8 and 9–10, then students apply it to Australia's rainfall map. Project it, or print it as a worksheet.
